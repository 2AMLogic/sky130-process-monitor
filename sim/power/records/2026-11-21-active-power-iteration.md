## 2. Decomposition at each binding cell (one single-corner probe per oscillator)

`2026-11-21-decompose.json`; probe reports `reports/probe_osc_*.json[.gz]`. Method: supply power over the
settled half-window from the `klt sim` `energy` measurement (net delivered energy ÷ window), cross-checked
against the committed mean-current convention (`P_iavg`) — the two agree to ≤ 0.1 % on every probe, and
each probe's `P_iavg` reproduces the committed table's cell to the same digits. The explicit-switching term
is `N · C · VDD · ΔV · f` with ΔV the measured post-RC swing (exact for a capacitor charged from a fixed
rail, independent of waveform shape). The DC term is the committed idle current at the same cell. The
residual holds intrinsic-device-capacitance switching and crowbar (overlap) current together — **this bench
does not separate them**, and the residual is not attributed to crowbar alone. A first-order size bound:
the fanout gate area per wire stage is ≈ (8+4) µm × 0.15 µm ≈ 1.8 µm², i.e. at Cox ≈ 8 fF/µm² (approximate
card value, not a recorded measurement) ≈ 6-8 fF of intrinsic capacitance against the 100 fF explicit cap,
so ≤ ~8 % of the explicit term can be intrinsic charging; the residual of the wire ring is therefore
dominated by overlap current driven by the slow RC input slopes.

| Probe (binding cell) | P_supply | P_explicit (31×100 fF) | P_dc (idle) | Residual (intrinsic + crowbar) | f | post-RC swing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `osc_wire` ff/1.98/−40 | 1740.2 µW | **1351.3 µW (77.6 %)** | 0.011 µW | 388.9 µW (22.3 %) | 110.778 MHz | 1.987 V |
| `osc_hd` ff/1.98/125 | 396.4 µW | 0 (no explicit caps) | 0.091 µW | 396.3 µW | 975.4 MHz | 2.186 V |
| `osc_nmos` ff/1.98/−40 | 351.5 µW | 0 | 0.010 µW | 351.5 µW | 380.6 MHz | 2.213 V |
| `osc_pmos` ff/1.98/125 | 165.6 µW | 0 | 0.037 µW | 165.6 µW | 142.9 MHz | 2.138 V |
| `osc_subth` ff/1.98/125 | **not measured this pass** (below) | 0 | includes the ~1 µA ungated divider | — | — | — |

Findings (all measured, none assumed):

- **Stage count is power-neutral — confirmed, not assumed.** Across wire (1743/1740/1738), nmos
  (352/351), pmos (166/166) and hd (395/396/398) the binding-cell power moves ≤ 0.4 % while frequency
  scales 1/N. First-order ring power ≈ C·V²/(2·t_stage) has no N in it; the measurement agrees. Stage
  count is a frequency lever only (counter coverage, quantization), never a power lever, on all four
  ring families. The full-box check (§4, `hd_n061`) confirms it across the corner box, not just one cell.
- **`osc_wire` is RC-delay-dominated, so the capacitor lever is also power-neutral.** Halving the
  explicit 100 fF load raises f nearly proportionally (110.8 → 184 MHz at 50 fF) and leaves P within 2 %:
  in the RC-dominated regime P = C·V²/(2·t) with t ∝ C. Removing explicit capacitance would not cut power
  — it would only erase the wire-RC signature the oscillator exists to provide.
- **The R length is the power lever that preserves the ring's purpose.** P falls monotonically with R
  (2281 → 1740 → 1227 → 848 µW for L = 500 → 1000 → 2000 → 4000 µm) while the RC fraction *rises*
  (0.51 → 0.69 → 0.82 → 0.90). Driver weakening also cuts P (688 µW at 2/1 µm) but erodes the RC
  fraction (0.33); it is best used in combination (r004w02: 447 µW with fraction 0.72).
- **For the three capless rings the residual is slope-driven overlap current.** P/f grows as the ring
  slows (nmos: 0.92 → 1.17 → 1.66 → 2.15 µW/MHz from base → l30): the weak long-channel device plus its
  own slow input slope lengthens the both-on window. Lengthening the weak device is the lever that cuts
  power on both terms (lower f and lower overlap conductance).
- **`osc_hd` has no sizing lever at all.** It is the standard-cell baseline (nand2_1 + inv_1); its stage
  capacitance and delay are the library cell's own. The only free parameter is stage count, which is
  power-neutral (above). Its power is fixed by the cell library at ~396 µW worst-cell — see §5.

## 5. Candidate or limiting mechanism, per over-target oscillator

- **`osc_wire`** — candidate **`r004w02`** (per-stage R L = 4000 µm, drivers W = 2/1 µm, 31 stages, 31 ×
  100 fF): 447.2 µW worst-cell, under the 500 µW **fallback** at every graded cell, RC fraction 0.72 at
  the binding cell (base 0.69), counter-covered with 5-18× margin. Device count **unchanged**: 31
  capacitors + 31 resistors (each resistor 4× longer, W 0.14 µm — this informs #26's LVS/area planning
  and the #75 serpentine packing; the wire cell's 12 257 µm² footprint grows accordingly). Limiting
  mechanism toward the 100 µW target: P ≈ C·V²/(2·t_stage) with t_stage RC-dominated needs t_stage ≈ 15×
  the committed value (R ≈ 15 kµm per stage) — implausible area, and driver-only weakening collapses the
  RC fraction below 0.35. The target is not reachable for this oscillator while it keeps its wire-RC
  sensing role.
- **`osc_hd`** — no candidate; mechanism: power is fixed by the standard cell (inv_1) and invariant to
  stage count (measured across the box). Worst cell 396-398 µW at every stage count. Any reduction means
  leaving standard cells (a different design decision) or accepting it; it alone keeps the bank over the
  100 µW target forever, so **the row-7 target (< 100 µW) is infeasible for the committed bank
  composition** — only the fallback row is closable, by `osc_wire` → `r004w02` above.
- **`osc_nmos`** — candidate recorded but **not proposed**: `l30` reaches 102.1 µW worst-cell (2 % over
  the 100 µW target, under fallback everywhere), but its cost is the erosion of the Pn sensitivity column
  it exists to provide (0.157 → 0.050 at ss/1.62/125). Limiting mechanism: the L lever trades power
  against the ring's process signature; past ~L = 2 µm the trade stops paying. `osc_nmos` stays as
  committed (351.5 µW worst-cell, under the fallback) unless the human decision accepts the sensitivity
  cost.
- **`osc_pmos`** — candidate **`l10`** (pull-up L 0.5 → 1.0 µm): 81.7 µW worst-cell, **under the 100 µW
  target at every graded cell**, counter-covered, sensitivity row substantially unchanged. Proposed in
  decision 0004.
