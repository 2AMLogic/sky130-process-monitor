# 2026-11-05 — osc_hd grid and mismatch MC re-run with a 5 ps max step (issue #54)

**SIMULATED. Schematic-level netlists (`design/osc_bank/` osc_hd, 31-stage inverter ring), no layout/parasitics, ngspice on the sky130A models via the `klt sim` batch fleet. Nothing measured.** **No estimate accuracy is quoted and no margin is recomputed** (the solve procedure has not been run against these data). Append-only: no earlier request, report or record is edited. No spec change.

Why: `2026-11-05-mc-offnom-partial.md` section 1 showed a 20 ps `tran` step locks the osc_hd period to the step grid (ff nominal sigma(ln f) = 0.00000) and biases it (~11 % at ff, ~3 % at tt in a local single-corner probe). This record re-runs the three osc_hd request sets with the max step capped at 5 ps and compares.

All 5 x 3 cells are n/N = 50/50 or 30/30.

Pooled sigma(ln f) (RMS over corners, computed by `analyze.mc_sigmas` per cell; cross-cell pools computed by hand from the table values, 5 s.f. rounding of the inputs):
- nominal cell: coarse 0.00336 (0.00375 excluding the ff artefact) -> refined **0.00363**.
- 1.62 V/125 C: coarse 0.00316 -> refined 0.00318.
- 1.98 V/-40 C: coarse 0.00479 -> refined 0.00304 (driven by the fs outlier).
- **osc_hd pooled over all 15 (corner, cell) values: refined 0.00329**; coarse 0.00397 over the 14 non-artefact values (0.00384 if the ff nominal zero is kept in the 15).

So: the ff zero-sigma artefact is gone at the nominal cell (0.00241 at n=50). The one other large coarse discrepancy was fs at 1.98 V/-40 C (0.00749 -> 0.00226); the earlier record's remark that 1.98 V/-40 C is "1.43x nominal for osc_hd" is therefore not supported by the refined data (refined cold/nominal pooled = 0.84x, hot/nominal = 0.88x, both inside sampling error). Nominal-sigma-everywhere stays untested only for osc_nmos / osc_wire (outside this issue).

Mean ln f per cell moved up with the finer step too (nominal cell: ff +0.108 = +11.4 %, fs +0.037, sf +0.032, ss +0.017, tt +0.032 in ln f), consistent with section 2 and with the ~11 % ff / ~3 % tt probe in the earlier record.

## 4. What this does and does not say
- Every coarse-step osc_hd frequency in the existing records is biased low by roughly 0.1-2.4 % (grid, 10 ps) and ~2-11 % (MC means, 20 ps). The refined reports are the better osc_hd inputs; the sensitivity matrices, conditioning and discrimination margin were **not recomputed** here and no P/V/T estimate or accuracy is claimed. That recomputation is a separate step.
- Not checked: step sensitivity of osc_nmos / osc_pmos / osc_wire (still open, flagged in the earlier record); remaining #48 runs (osc_nmos, osc_wire, cold osc_subth, per-(V,T) margin) are untouched.
- Resolution of what 5 ps itself leaves: no 2.5 ps run was made, so residual step bias at 5 ps is unmeasured.
