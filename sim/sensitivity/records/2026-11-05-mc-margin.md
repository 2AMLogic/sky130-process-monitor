## Run notes (reported, not hidden)
- All 5 requests completed on the fleet. One nmos shard (`klt-sim-e9af6963f8ae`) ran 3008 s and ended exit 4 (one unit hit the 3000 s `timeout_s`; it is not a spot interruption). I stopped the stalled `klt sim --batch-set` client after ~60 min and collected with `--attach` on the shard map. Result: osc_nmos ss has 49 of 50 samples, osc_subth sf 19 of 20 (non-oscillating samples are counted as missing, not filled).
- MC covers **only the nominal cell (1.80 V / 27 C)**, not the three (V,T) cells named in the issue. The per-unit fleet wall time made 750 units per oscillator impractical; the generator already documents this scoping. The off-nominal MC cells remain open work.
- The margin below applies the nominal-cell pooled sigma(ln f) to every (V,T) cell. That is an assumption, not a measurement. Sigma at 1.62 V/125 C and 1.98 V/-40 C is unknown and could be larger (especially subthreshold).
- Anomaly, unexplained: osc_hd at corner ff shows sigma(ln f) = 0.00000 (spread ~3e-7 relative, numerical noise) while the other corners show ~0.003-0.004 and osc_nmos ff shows 0.0026. I have not determined why mismatch has no effect there. It lowers osc_hd's pooled sigma and I did not correct for it.
- osc_subth MC is **UNVALIDATED** (mismatch switches in subthreshold are #10's question). Its pooled sigma(ln f) = 0.266 (0.12 to 0.32 per corner) is two orders larger than the other four oscillators (0.0015 to 0.0067).

Discrimination margin = min over corner pairs of centroid distance / noise along the pair line; noise = counter quantization (2^10 ref cycles @ 10 MHz) + pooled MC sigma in quadrature. Only the 5 formed cells have a margin.

| bank | form | min margin | where |
|---|---|---|---|
| all 5 oscillators | absolute | **1.13** | V=1.98 T=27, pair (tt, fs) |
| all 5 oscillators | ratio-only | **0.46** | V=1.98 T=125, pair (tt, sf) |
| without osc_subth | absolute | 8.02 | V=1.80 T=125, pair (tt, fs) |
| without osc_subth | ratio-only | 4.22 | V=1.80 T=27, pair (tt, fs) |

The spec's decision rule (spec §2) sets no numeric threshold; it says the margin is to be derived. This record therefore does not call any margin sufficient.
Without osc_subth, 6 of the 10 matrices formed (5 (V,T) cells x absolute/ratio-only) are rank 3 of 4 (kappa = inf in 5, 1.97e+03 in one), i.e. the P/V/T columns are then not separable there. With it, rank is 4 of 4 but the margin collapses because of its MC spread. The subthreshold oscillator is both what makes the system full-rank and what limits the margin. Whether that spread is real or a model artefact is #10.
