## 1. Why osc_hd ff showed sigma(ln f) = 0.00000 (measured, local single-corner probe)

Result: **mismatch IS applied at ff; the zero is a time-step artefact of the 20 ps `tran` step.** At the nominal ff cell the oscillation period sits on the step grid and the measured crossings do not move, whatever the device draw.

Evidence (all local `ngspice -b`, one deck per corner, PDK open_pdks c6d73a35, 1.80 V, 27 C, `.param mc_mm_switch=1` after the `.lib`, `set rndseed=N` + `reset` per sample):
- Netlist/model check: the ff and tt pm3 model files carry the same `MC_MM_SWITCH*AGAUSS(0,1,1)*(slope/sqrt(l*w*mult))` terms on vth0/toxe/voff for nfet_01v8 and pfet_01v8_hvt (the inverter's pull-up in `sky130_fd_sc_hd__inv_1`); `mc_mm_switch` is set by the `.lib` section name (`ff` -> 0) and the request's `.param` override turns it on.
- Per-instance parameters differ by seed at ff exactly as at tt: single `inv_1`, 3 seeds, effective nfet vth0 9.2967 / 9.2957 / 9.3190 (ff) vs 5.0495 / 5.0217 / 5.0244 (tt), pfet vth0 -0.3817 / -0.3592 / -0.3363 (ff). DC supply current of one `inv_1`, 4 seeds: ff 11.25 / 11.67 / 9.68 / 10.74 uA; tt 3.84 / 4.14 / 5.06 / 4.01 uA.
- The full 31-stage osc_hd, `tran 20p 60n`, 6 seeds: average supply current varies per seed at ff (0.1310 to 0.1324 mA), yet the period between rising edge 10 and edge 30 is constant to better than 5e-5 relative (13.4514 to 13.4520 ns edge-10 time, 1.240 ns period). Same deck at tt varies (edge-10 time 16.88 to 17.07 ns).
- With the step refined (`tran 5p 40n 0 5p`, i.e. max step 5 ps) ff period varies by seed and is shorter: edge-10/edge-30 times 12.211/34.511, 12.222/34.545, 12.200/34.482 ns -> periods 1.115, 1.116, 1.114 ns (computed as `(t30 - t10)/20`), vs 1.240 ns at 20 ps (11 % long). So at 20 ps the ff period is both locked and ~10 % long.
- The same 5 ps refinement at tt: edge-10/edge-30 16.4435/47.2050, 16.5046/47.397, 16.4422/47.2024 ns -> period 1.538, 1.545, 1.537 ns, vs about 1.59 ns at 20 ps (~3 %).
- Consistent with the fleet data: at the two off-nominal cells (same 20 ps request) osc_hd ff sigma is **not** zero: 0.00204 (1.62 V/125 C) and 0.00294 (1.98 V/-40 C). The lock occurs at the particular (corner, V, T) whose period falls on the grid.

Consequences (stated, not fixed here):
- The nominal-cell osc_hd ff sigma = 0 is an artefact and should be excluded from osc_hd's pooled sigma: pooled over the other four nominal corners, RMS of (0.00290, 0.00379, 0.00430, 0.00389) = 0.00375 (vs 0.00336 with the artefact; arithmetic from the 2026-11-05 record's table).
- The 20 ps step on osc_hd also biases the grid frequencies (period error ~11 % at ff and ~3 % at tt, at these two points); the whole osc_hd row of the sensitivity matrices and the other fast oscillators' windows (nmos 40p, pmos/wire 100p/50p steps vs their periods) have not been checked against step refinement. That is open work (new issue), not asserted either way.
- A fix would add a max step to `analysis.args` (e.g. `"5p 100n 0 5p"`) and re-run the osc_hd grid and MC; not done here (the grid record is append-only evidence and the cost is a fleet re-run).
