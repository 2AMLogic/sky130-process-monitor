# sim/subthreshold — spec Q2: are the open sky130 models good enough in subthreshold?

**Everything here is SIMULATED** (ngspice 46 on the batch fleet, sky130A models, `models_lib_sha256`
`17c208a6…`, identical to the pinned open_pdks `c6d73a35…` file on this host). Nothing is measured.
Each result names its corner, supply, temperature and testbench. Records: `measurements/subthreshold_records.jsonl`
(append-only). Raw `klt sim` reports: `runs/` (gzip). Reduction: `python3 -I analyze.py` (prints every table below;
`--append` writes the records, once).

Other observations (all simulated):

- **Flat leakage floor.** Every device and corner bottoms at the same ~1.09e-14 A at Vgs=0 (cold ss/fs/sf), independent of
  width and type: a numerical/model floor, not device leakage. With ngspice's default `gmin` the floor was ~9.1e-13 A and
  corrupted the cold slopes (e.g. S = 62 874 mV/dec); the first probe run is kept as `runs/probe_gmin1e-12.report.json.gz`.
- **Slope.** S at tt 27 °C is 84 (nfet) / 137–154 (pfet) / 118 (pfet_lvt) mV/dec; the pfet and pfet_lvt slopes grow ×2.2–2.8 from
  −40 to 125 °C, faster than T. Whether that is a model artefact needs a reference not available here.
- **Width dependence.** Ioff/W at W 0.84–0.86 µm vs 0.42–0.44 µm (tt, 27 °C): nfet 0.90, pfet 4.27, pfet_lvt 1.60. The pfet
  has no model bin below W=0.42 µm (a W=0.40 instance fails with "could not find a valid modelname"), and the oscillator's
  W=0.42 and 0.84 devices sit exactly on bin edges.
- **Supply.** f(1.98 V)/f(1.62 V) = 2.57 at tt 27 °C (the header bias divider tracks Vdd). Input for #8, not judged here.
- **Mismatch (`mc_mm_switch`=1, N=30 each, tt 1.80 V).** Frequency spread σ/mean: 29.1 % at −40 °C (5n/400u, 30/30 oscillate),
  13.4 % at 27 °C (2n/80u), 10.0 % at 125 °C (0.2n/3u). Mean is below the no-mismatch value (−19 %, −13 %, −3 %).
  Control `mismatch_scale 0` (N=5): all five samples identical (1 706 830 Hz), so the spread is the switch, not noise.
  The first N=30 at −40 °C with `2n/80u` had 1/30 samples complete the measurement: window, not mismatch.
  `mc_pr_switch` was not used.

### Method findings (change how `sim/osc_bank` numbers above a few MHz should be read)

- **The `.tran 2n 80u` bench is unreliable above ~5 MHz.** ff/1.80 V/125 °C gave 142.6 MHz at 2 ns step and 36.0–36.3 MHz at
  0.2 ns / 0.1 ns (local 0.1n 1u vs batch 0.2n 3u agree to 1 %). tt/1.8 V/125 °C: 19.1 MHz vs 12.7 MHz. Fine-step values
  replace the coarse ones in the table; the first-grid record is kept (append-only), flagged by `tran_step_window`. Cells still
  at 2n step are ≤ 5.2 MHz; the 7.4 MHz cell moved by 6 %, so their error is not quantified here but is bounded by that order.
- The 80 µs window is too short for f < ~140 kHz (11 periods): 12 first-grid cells failed that way, 5 resolved at 400 µs.

## Issue #44 follow-up (simulated, ngspice 47 on the batch fleet; the first run was ngspice 46)

Records: `record` = `osc_grid_cold_2ms`, `osc_cold_edges`, `device_probe_solver_sweep` in `measurements/subthreshold_records.jsonl`
(appended; nothing existing altered). All four requests went through `klt sim --backend batch --wait-for-capacity 600`; none ran locally.

**Cold/slow cells, `5n 2m` (testbench `osc_subth_tb.spice`).** Validation cell sf 1.80 V/27 °C: 37 437.0 Hz at `5n 2m` vs 37 437.0 Hz at
`5n 400u` (0.00 % apart; tolerance 1 %). Of the 7 unresolved cells:

| Cell | Result in 2 ms |
|---|---|
| ss 1.98 V, −40 °C | **resolved: 7 559.7 Hz** (edges 3 and 11) |
| sf 1.62 V, 27 °C | **resolved: 18 776.8 Hz** |
| ss 1.80 V, −40 °C | oscillates; edge 1 at 331 µs, edge 2 at 659 µs, edge 3 at 987 µs; no 11th edge. Edge spacing ≈ 3.0 kHz (not an f_osc measurement) |
| ss 1.62 V, −40 °C | oscillates; edges at 824 µs and 1 624 µs; no 3rd edge. Spacing ≈ 1.25 kHz |
| sf 1.98 V, −40 °C | oscillates; edges at 1.003 ms and 1.948 ms; spacing ≈ 1.06 kHz |
| sf 1.80 V, −40 °C | one rising edge (1.943 ms); swing −1e-6..1.69 V |
| sf 1.62 V, −40 °C | **no rising edge through 0.9 V in 2 ms**; v(out) peaks at 1.38 V. Still start-up-limited or not oscillating: not distinguished |

So 2 more cells resolve (40 of 45), 3 cells are shown to oscillate with periods of ~0.3–1 ms (the 400 µs window was simply too short; no f_osc because the 11th edge is past 2 ms),
**sf 1.62 V/−40 °C stays unresolved** (no oscillation vs. too slow), and sf 1.80 V/−40 °C shows one edge, which does not give f. Those 5
are reported as "no f_osc in 2 ms", not extended. The spacing-based frequencies are coarse and are not used in any table above.
Where they oscillate, f at −40 °C is of order 1–3 kHz, well below the ≥ 27.5 kHz that a 400 µs window can resolve; the readout's gate time is not judged here.

**Leakage floor.** Probe re-run with `gmin` 1e-15 / 1e-17 / 1e-18 and `abstol` 1e-18 (all other settings as `probe.request.json`):

| Cold cell, Vgs=0, W=0.42 | gmin 1e-15 | gmin 1e-17 | gmin 1e-18 | gmin 1e-15 + abstol 1e-18 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ss −40 °C, pfet | 1.0907e-14 A | 1.0017e-14 | 1.0008e-14 | 1.0907e-14 |
| sf −40 °C, pfet | 1.0906e-14 | 1.0015e-14 | 1.0006e-14 | 1.0906e-14 |

The part of the floor that tracks `gmin` is `gmin × Vds` (9.0e-16 A at 1e-15, matches the 8.9e-16 A drop). What remains,
**1.000e-14 A, does not move with `gmin` (1e-15 → 1e-18) and `abstol` has no effect at all** (bit-identical to the `gmin` 1e-15 run). So the
~1.09e-14 A floor is 1.0e-14 A of something we cannot change from the request, plus the `gmin` term. The origin of that 1.0e-14 A is not
identified here (a constant in the model or the engine; not traced). It does **not** matter at the oscillator's own currents: the header
pfet_lvt Id at |Vsg| 0.30 V, tt 27 °C is 4.03994e-08 A at every setting (identical to 6 digits). At the cold corners the floor is 1.0e-14 A
against 6.2e-11 A (ss, lvt, 0.30 V) and 5.0e-12 A (sf, lvt): ≤ 0.2 %. It only distorts the plain pfet at those corners (Id(0.30 V) 1.4e-14 A at ss, 1.09e-14 A at sf), which is why check 3
excludes them.

**pfet S(T) reference (check 3): none found.** Searched: the repo (`spec/`, `WORK_PLAN.md`, this README) and the PDK install used here
(`~/.ciel/sky130A`: `libs.tech/ngspice`, `libs.ref/sky130_fd_pr/spice`). The PDK ships BSIM4 model cards (`pfet_01v8`, `pfet_01v8_lvt` `.pm3.spice`
and corner files) but no measured or documented S(T) data, and no documentation files. **Needed data (a human-hands step, not available in
this repo):** foundry or silicon subthreshold characteristics of the pfet and pfet_lvt over −40..125 °C. Check 3 stays **FAIL** with its 1.2–2.2 band
unchanged; the model fault vs. check fault question stays open.

**5 MHz bench limit.** Audit of numbers above ~5 MHz that used the 2n step:
`sim/subthreshold`: only one cell, **fs 1.80 V/−40 °C = 5 194.6 kHz (`2n/80u`) is step-limited** (the 7.4 MHz cell at 2n moved by 6 % when re-run at
0.2n, so treat this one as ±6 % or worse). Every other cell above 5 MHz is already a fine-step (`0.2n/3u`) value (table above). In `sim/osc_bank`,
`measurements/osc_bank_records.jsonl` has a single `osc_subth` record (tt 1.80 V/27 °C, 1.71 MHz at 2n/80u, below the limit); the other four oscillator benches
use `.tran 5p 400n`. `sim/osc_bank/bench_osc_subth.spice:17` still has `.tran 2n 80u`: **flagged, not edited** (design bench, out of scope here).
The step-limited cell was not re-run at 0.2n; it is listed as step-limited.

## Verdict: INCONCLUSIVE (model-usable at tt/ff/fs ≥ 27 °C; cold slow corners narrowed by #44, one cell unresolved)

- Supports "model is self-consistent": checks 1, 2, 4, 5 pass; frequency follows the header current's temperature
  dependence uniformly across five corners; the mismatch switch acts in subthreshold with a clean control.
- Supports "model is suspect": check 3 fails (subthreshold ideality grows with T faster than the model's own 1/T scaling would
  suggest for the pfet devices that set the oscillator current) and a device-independent leakage floor of ~1.1e-14 A exists.
- Supports neither: after #44, 5 of 45 spec-box cells have no f_osc (3 shown to oscillate at ~1–3 kHz spacing, 1 with a single edge, sf 1.62 V/−40 °C unresolved) at the slow/cold end (was 7), which is exactly where the spec's
  temperature row needs the oscillator.
- Not "design-limited": the oscillator runs and is monotonic in T and V wherever it resolves; its T sensitivity (×7.4 over
  27→125 °C at tt) is large. Supply sensitivity is also large (×2.57 for ±10 % V) — a #8 matter.

**Evidence that would settle it (status after #44):** (1) *done in part:* the cold cells re-run at 2 ms — 2 resolved, 3 shown oscillating
at 1–3 kHz edge spacing, 1 with a single edge, 1 (sf 1.62 V/−40 °C) still unresolved; a faster-start enable is not tested (it would change the oscillator); (2) a reference for subthreshold S(T) of the sky130 pfet / pfet_lvt (foundry data or silicon) to judge check 3;
(3) *done in part:* the floor is 1.0e-14 A independent of `gmin`/`abstol` plus `gmin`·Vds; its origin is still unidentified, and it does not matter at the oscillator's own currents; original wording: the origin of the 1.09e-14 A floor (model parameter vs ngspice) and whether it matters at the oscillator's own currents
(header pfet_lvt Id ≈ 4.0e-8 A at \|Vsg\| 0.30 V, 27 °C, tt, W=0.42, Vds 0.9 V). No spec row or oscillator definition is changed by this verdict, so no decision
record is raised; the unresolved cold cells and the 5 MHz bench limit are for follow-up issues.
